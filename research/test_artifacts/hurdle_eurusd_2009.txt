The point of this run is the LAST table in each block. Assuming the truth is a
zero edge, the best of N noisy estimates lands about z*SE above zero, and z
grows with log(N). That is how many parameter combinations it would take for a
search to produce a configuration that APPEARS to clear a realistic cost while
being pure selection noise.

On M5 that number is around 500 tries, which is an entirely plausible amount
of threshold tuning. On H1, H4 and D1 it is about 10 tries. Anyone who tunes ten
configurations on a 2,708-outcome sample will find one that looks profitable,
and it will mean nothing.

The 95% confidence intervals matter for the same reason. On H1 the measured
+0.0347R sits inside -0.026R..+0.095R. On D1 the +0.1150R sits inside
-0.126R..+0.356R, on 122 resolved outcomes. Neither is distinguishable from a
coin at the sample sizes available.
