PriceActionBarByBar - FIXED 1:2 payoff at 0.5% account risk, EURUSD 2009
Phase 20 follow-up, produced by research/pab_fixed_rr.py.

A RESEARCH-ONLY sensitivity analysis, and labelled as one. It is NOT a
canonical measurement and NOT a profitability claim. The engine places no
orders; neither does this.

WHY IT EXISTS. The engine sets its own levels: a baseline target of
entry +/- 2.0 * bar.range, a clamp toward context support/resistance, and a
gate rejecting anything below 1.50 reward/risk. So R:R in an export is
VARIABLE, and the win rate that goes with it is not reusable at a different
payoff. The most likely misuse of this project's numbers is exactly that:
take the 47.7% M5 win rate and multiply it by a 2:1 payoff.

METHOD. Only the TARGET is replaced, with entry +/- 2 * risk. The stop is the
engine's own invalidation, unchanged, and R is defined by the stop distance,
the same definition TradingCost.mqh uses. Exits are re-simulated from the
exported bars. Two concurrency policies are reported because they are not the
same experiment: 'every event' is the report's per-event expectancy and is not
an account, while 'one position at a time' is the only one of the two that is a
real balance curve.

CAVEATS, all of which matter more than the headline:
  - Every net figure uses an ASSUMED round-trip spread, supplied here. No
    measured spread exists; the Strategy Tester exposes none (error 4807).
  - Slippage and commission are excluded, so a real cost is larger still.
  - The bar export carries no close price, so an expired trade cannot be
    valued exactly. Expiries are counted per timeframe above.
  - One market, one year, one broker, 2009.
  - The 'one at a time' filter selects a different subset than the resolved
    set, so its win rate differs from the headline figure. Both are shown.
