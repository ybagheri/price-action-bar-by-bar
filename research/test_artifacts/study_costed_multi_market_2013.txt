=======================================================================
 COST MEASUREMENT - A FINDING, NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION DETAIL
=======================================================================
The exporter now measures per-bar spread from the terminal, and records
spread, slippage, commission, cost_r and a cost_model label on every row.
On this run every cost field came back BLANK, and that is the honest
result rather than a failure:

  PabEventExport: CopyBuffer copied -1 of 73752 spread values - error 4807
  PabEventExport: costs ASSUMED (iSpread unavailable)

The MT5 Strategy Tester exposes no historical spread buffer. iSpread's
CopyBuffer fails there with error 4807 and returns no values, so a
per-bar spread cannot be measured in this harness at all. An earlier
build wrote 0.0 into those columns, which would have handed the research
layer 73,751 rows of "trading was free" and published a net expectancy
that was really the gross one. The exporter now writes blanks, the loader
reads a blank as "no cost data", and every report says GROSS in those
words.

That is why the two analyses below exist. They answer the cost question
without inventing a spread.

Read the M5 numbers carefully: EURUSD M5's entire edge is worth 0.0003%
of price in round-trip cost. On EURUSD that is roughly one third of a pip
of total spread, slippage, and commission combined. No retail FX market
trades at that cost. The GBPUSD and USDJPY H1 figures, at 0.0019% and
0.0014%, are the largest in the study and still only a fraction of a pip
plus a pip.

=======================================================================
 WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
=======================================================================
The blocking gap from Phase 18 is closed, and it closes against the
project. The edge is not small-positive after costs. The edge is a
thousandth of a percent of price, and the cost of trading EURUSD at M5 is
several times that.

Phase 18 said: "this study cannot say whether the net figure is
small-positive or clearly negative." This study says: not small-positive.
At a thousandth of a percent of price the sample is already negative, and
no plausible retail spread is below that. The remaining uncertainty is
the direction and size of a cost that is comfortably larger than the
thing it is being subtracted from.

This is a stronger negative result than Phase 18's, and it did not require
a single new measurement of the engine. It required admitting that the
one number the whole project rested on could never survive contact with
a broker's cost sheet.

WHAT IS STILL MISSING:
  - A MEASURED spread. The exporter reads per-bar spread correctly on a
    live chart, where MT5 hands OnCalculate a real spread array. It cannot
    be read in the Strategy Tester, so a historical study on this harness
    cannot carry a measured cost. Obtaining one needs either live forward
    collection or exported tick data.
  - Slippage and commission remain ASSUMPTIONS, labelled as such on every
    row. They are not observable from a bar series by construction.
  - One broker feed, one year (the range the run reported: 2013).
  - M5 and H1 only. Nothing here says whether the rules do anything on
    H4 or D1.
  - The indicator lifecycle is still untested: no runtime coverage of
    OnCalculate or the chart renderer.

A NOTE ON THE HISTORICAL RANGE: this run reported 2013-01-01 to
2013-12-31, not the 2014 window requested. The cached history shifts
between runs, so any archived result must quote the range the run itself
printed.
