======================================================================
 THE HEADLINE, STATED PLAINLY
======================================================================
No timeframe shows an out-of-sample edge. M5 is flat, H1 is flat, H4 is
NEGATIVE in both in-sample and out-of-sample, and D1 looks positive only
until you split it.

  timeframe   resolved   win%   grossR   breakEvenSpread   bars to exit
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  EURUSD M5       31799  47.7%   +0.003   0.0001% of price         2.5
  EURUSD H1        2708  47.6%   +0.035   0.0060% of price         2.8
  EURUSD H4         678  42.6%   -0.068   n/a (gross < 0)           2.7
  EURUSD D1         122  48.4%   +0.115   0.1152% of price          3.3

H4 is the one group whose gross edge is NEGATIVE before a single unit of
cost is applied. Break-even is undefined for it because there is nothing to
break even against. That is a stronger negative than Phase 19's: it does not
depend on any cost assumption, and no plausible cost model can rescue it.

======================================================================
 WALK-FORWARD - 4 chronological folds, decisions never cross a boundary
======================================================================
  timeframe   in_sample   out_of_sample   degradation (OOS - IS)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  M5              -0.00R           +0.01R        +0.01R
  H1              +0.01R           +0.06R        +0.05R
  H4              -0.10R           -0.03R        +0.07R
  D1              +0.30R           -0.09R        -0.39R

D1 IS THE ONE TO WATCH. Its pooled +0.115R is the largest positive gross
figure anywhere in this project, and it is entirely an in-sample artefact:
+0.30R in-sample becomes -0.09R out-of-sample, a -0.39R degradation. That is
the textbook signature of fitting noise, and it is exactly what a 122-outcome
sample is entitled to produce. It is reported here so it cannot be mistaken
for a result. The report prints "reliable: yes" for D1, but that flag only
means the resolved count cleared the threshold; it is not a statement about
whether the edge is real, and on this evidence it is not.

  D1 per-fold gross: +0.51R, +0.01R, +0.14R, -0.17R
  H4 per-fold gross: -0.02R, +0.06R, -0.18R, -0.13R

H4 is negative in 3 of 4 folds and negative in both pooled halves. That is a
consistent absence of edge rather than a noisy one.

======================================================================
 RE-PRICED AT ASSUMED SPREADS - assumptions, not measurements
======================================================================
Spread only, as a percentage of price. Slippage and commission are excluded,
so a real cost is at least this large.

  assumed round-trip spread   M5        H1        H4        D1
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.000% of price          +0.003R  +0.035R  -0.068R  +0.115R
  0.001% of price          -0.038R  +0.025R  -0.073R  +0.114R
  0.005% of price          -0.201R  -0.015R  -0.091R  +0.108R

The H1 row is the most interesting of the four and is a change of emphasis
rather than a change of conclusion. At a thousandth of a percent of price,
H1 is still POSITIVE (+0.025R) where M5 is already clearly negative
(-0.038R). That is because H1's stop distance is larger in price terms, so a
given cost is a smaller fraction of R.

It should not be mistaken for a reason to prefer H1. Two things bound it:
H1's edge is +0.035R gross and its break-even is 0.0060% of price, so the
margin over a realistic spread is thin and rests on a single broker, a
single market and a single year. And at 0.005% it is already negative. A
result that survives one cost scenario and not the next is not a result.

======================================================================
 WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
======================================================================
This is ONE market and ONE year. It does not widen the study in the way
Phase 18 widened it, because three of the four symbols could not be run at
H4/D1 within the available history, and the reason is worth recording.

- **The other three markets are missing.** Only EURUSD was measured on the
  swing timeframes. GBPUSD, USDCHF and USDJPY are absent, so "H4 is
  negative" is a statement about EURUSD H4 in 2009, not about H4.
- **2009 is not 2013.** Phase 19's M5/H1 study covers 2013. This covers
  2009. The two are NOT poolable and no figure here should be compared with
  a Phase 19 figure as though the market period were held constant.
- **The cost model is still unmeasured.** Every cost figure above is GROSS,
  and every scenario row is an assumption supplied on the command line. No
  measured spread exists. See the spread probe note at the end.
- **D1's sample is 122 resolved outcomes.** It cannot settle a question about
  a timeframe on its own, and the walk-forward above says its positive
  figure does not survive.
- **Nothing here says H4 or D1 are worse than M5 in general.** It says that on
  this market, in this year, neither showed an out-of-sample edge, and H4
  showed a negative gross expectancy.

======================================================================
 A FINDING THAT CONTRADICTS ROADMAP ITEM 5
======================================================================
Roadmap item 5 rests on the claim that "H1 setups take 24-34 bars to exit
with slightly negative expectancy, suggesting the stop and target logic is
not doing anything on that timeframe."

On this sample that does not reproduce. H1 exits in 2.8 bars and is
+0.035R gross, and bars-to-exit is 2.4 to 3.3 on EVERY timeframe including
D1. The same engine and the same parameter fingerprint that produced
Phase 19's 28.9-bar H1 figure produce 2.8 bars here.

Same engine (1.50), same parameters, same market (EURUSD H1), different
year. So the long H1 holding period is a property of the 2013 SAMPLE, not a
structural property of H1 setup construction. Whatever produced 24-34 bars
there is not reproducible from the H1 timeframe alone, and "the stop and
target logic is not doing anything on H1" is not supported by this evidence.

What the code actually does, for the record, and without any change to it:

  - `evaluate_setup` in research/pab_research/outcomes.py has NO maximum
    holding horizon. It walks forward until the target or the invalidation is
    touched, and returns "expired" only when the data runs out. bars_to_exit
    is therefore purely a consequence of where the levels sit.
  - The baseline target is `entry +/- 2.0 * bar.range`
    (MQL5/Include/PriceActionBarByBar/DecisionEngine.mqh:166) - two times the
    range of the single bar the decision was taken on.
  - That target is then CLAMPED toward context resistance or support
    (DecisionEngine.mqh:185-201) before the reward/risk gate rejects anything
    below the 1.50 minimum.

Because the target is anchored to one bar's range, the holding period in
BARS is roughly timeframe-invariant, which is what the 2.4-3.3 column shows.
The holding period in TIME is not: 2.5 M5 bars is about 12 minutes and 3.3 D1
bars is about a week. That is a real asymmetry between the timeframes and it
is not visible in any figure reported so far, because the report counts bars
and not hours. It is worth knowing and it is NOT acted on here.

No threshold was tuned in response to any of this.

======================================================================
 WHY ONLY ONE MARKET: the tester agent's history is not selectable
======================================================================
Widening to H4/D1 hit a platform limitation that is easy to mistake for
missing data, so it is recorded precisely.

The Strategy Tester's own FromDate/ToDate do NOT bound what the exporter
replays. A run configured for 2012-2014 with the exporter's date inputs left
empty reported "EURUSD H4, 1546 bars from 2011-01-03 to 2011-12-30" - a
window that does not intersect the one requested. The tester's range drives
the tick stream only.

The agent also does not hold every timeframe for every year, and which year
it serves drifts between runs on the same machine and broker:

  unbounded, 2026-09-30 08:56    H4  1546 bars  2011-01-03 .. 2011-12-30
  unbounded, 2026-09-30 09:0x    M5 72513 bars  2010-01-04 .. 2010-12-31
  pinned 2009,  2026-09-30 11:0x  M5 71817 bars  2009-01-02 .. 2009-12-31

Pinning the window to 2013 made all eight H4/D1 runs report zero bars. Left
unbounded, each run served a different year, so the four timeframes could not
be placed on a common period. Pinning to 2009 worked, and is why this study
is a single year: 2009 is a year the agent can serve on all four timeframes
for EURUSD.

The exporter's failure message was changed in this phase as a direct result.
It used to say "Check that the broker actually has history", which is a guess
about the cause and was wrong every time it fired. It now prints the span the
agent actually offered, so an empty window and a genuinely empty cache are
distinguishable from the output instead of inferred from prose.

======================================================================
 COST MEASUREMENT - STILL NOT MEASURED, AND NOW KNOWN WHY
======================================================================
Phase 20 item 1 was to obtain a measured spread. It is BLOCKED on this
machine, and the reason is established rather than assumed. The probe is
committed at MQL5/Experts/PabSpreadProbe.mq5 and its output is archived at
research/test_artifacts/spread_probe_20260930.txt.

  - `iSpread` + `CopyBuffer` still fails with error 4807, reconfirmed on
    2026-09-30. Phase 19's finding stands.
  - `CopyTicksRange` cannot substitute. In the tester it is clipped to the
    tester's CURRENT time: querying 2013.01.02..2013.01.03 from a run sitting
    at 2013.01.02 00:05 returned exactly ONE tick, the one that woke OnTick.
  - Real tick mode does not engage. The tester log for the real-ticks run
    says "EURUSD,M5: 3145 ticks, 842 bars generated". Requesting real ticks
    made the tick count 132x WORSE than the 416,989 of the generated run.
  - There are no per-day tick files anywhere under bases\...\ticks\<symbol>\.
    Only the ticks.dat index exists, so there is no cached tick history to
    reconstruct from.
  - ACCOUNT_TRADE_MODE reads 0 inside the tester, so an agent cannot reach the
    broker to download ticks, and a /config run does not trigger the
    download the terminal UI would.

So the bid and ask the tester supplies are SYNTHESISED from OHLC and the
spread inside them is a tester SETTING. The 35.0-point spread the probe
reports is that setting, reported faithfully. Writing it into a cost column
as "measured" is the exact failure this project exists to prevent, so the
blank cost columns are left alone and every expectancy here says GROSS.

Live forward collection remains possible in principle - the terminal IS
authorized to Alpari-MT5-Demo - but it needs a chart running for days or
weeks and it cannot cover 2009 retrospectively, so it would measure a
different period than the one these events come from.
