  07:30:58.088   RESULT: 51 passed, 3 failed
  07:31:04.903   RESULT: 51 passed, 3 failed
  07:31:29.756   RESULT: 51 passed, 3 failed
  07:31:41.088   RESULT: 51 passed, 3 failed

The three failures in each of those runs were:

  [FAIL] A symmetric move up and down produces equal-magnitude slopes
      Cause: asserted MathAbs(rising - falling) when the property is
             equal magnitude, i.e. MathAbs(rising + falling).
  [FAIL] Slope equals price fraction divided by bars elapsed
      Cause: expected 0.001, which silently treated the reference price as
             1.0. The correct value is (0.0100 / 1.1000) / 10.
  [FAIL] Identical percentage geometry gives an identical slope at 1.10 and at 3400
      Cause: used a 3400 -> 3427 move (+0.794%) against a 1.10 -> 1.11
             move (+0.909%). Fixed to 3400 * 1.1100 / 1.1000.

The four behavioural assertions added in the same phase - triangle
detection at 1.10 scale, triangle detection at 3400 scale, rejection of
widening structure, and rejection of untimed swings - passed in every run,
including these failing ones.
