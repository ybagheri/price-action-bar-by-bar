real engine defect rather than a bad fixture. mmUsable required only

ignored and the baseline target kept, exactly as a too-close resistance clamp
already was. Fixed in DecisionEngine.mqh; the assertion now pins the correct
behaviour, that the setup survives and the baseline target is used.
