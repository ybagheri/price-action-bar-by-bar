====================================================
 HONEST READING - what this sample does and does not show
====================================================
The engine has NO measurable edge on this sample. Every resolved expectancy
sits between -0.06R and +0.04R, and win rates sit between 38 percent and
55 percent. Walk-forward degradation is +0.01R and +0.9 percentage points,
which means the in-sample and out-of-sample halves performed the same, not
that either was good.

These are descriptive counts of one symbol, one timeframe, one year, and one
parameter set. They are NOT a profitability claim and must not be read as
one. Specifically excluded: spread, slippage, and commission, which the
export does not carry. Realistic costs on EURUSD M5 would consume several
times the measured per-trade edge, so the net result is worse than shown,
not better.

Two structural observations that matter more than the numbers above:

1. The setups are micro scalps. Average bars-to-exit is 1.9 to 2.8 for
   every setup type except breakout_follow_through at 10.8. The stop is
   the signal bar's own extreme plus 0.25 bar range, and the target is the
   nearest resistance or support. An event that resolves within two bars
   cannot distinguish skill from noise, and the ambiguous counts (486 of
   12,654 for failed_breakout) show how often both levels are touched in
   the same bar, which is unresolvable at M5.

2. One year of a single symbol on a single timeframe is not a study. The
   roadmap item for multi-instrument and multi-regime validation is still
   open, and this run is the first real evidence the measurement pipeline
   works, not evidence that the rules work.

What this run DOES establish: the export, the loader, the outcome
evaluator, the grouping, and the walk-forward split now run end to end on
real broker history, and every figure above is reproducible from the two
CSVs with one command.
