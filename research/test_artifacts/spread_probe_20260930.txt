################ tester log, verbatim ################

Both runs report 842 bars 'generated'. The tick counts are the sharper evidence:

  Model=0 (Every tick)   EURUSD,M5: 416989 ticks, 842 bars generated
  Model=2 (real ticks)   EURUSD,M5:   3145 ticks, 842 bars generated

Asking for REAL ticks made the tick count 132x WORSE, not better. Real broker
tick data for three days of EURUSD is millions of ticks; 3,145 ticks is a
coarse fallback, roughly four per M5 bar. So real tick mode did not engage.
It degraded instead of failing loudly, which is why the tick count, and not the
absence of an error, is the thing worth recording.

Consequence, and it is the whole point of this artifact: the bid and ask the
tester supplies are SYNTHESISED from OHLC, and the spread inside them is a
tester SETTING rather than a measurement of the broker. The 35.0-point spread
in the Model=0 output is that setting, reported faithfully. Recording it as a
measured spread would be the exact failure this project exists to prevent, so
Phase 19's blank cost columns remain correct and are left alone.
